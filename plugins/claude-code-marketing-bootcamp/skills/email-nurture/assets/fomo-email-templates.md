## Drafting notes (not part of the verbatim template)

- These templates contain em-dashes and rule-of-three by design — they are the
  proven Ship 30 originals. When adapting for a brand with a strict AI-pattern
  bar, run the AI-pattern cleanse (`references/patterns.md`) AFTER filling
  brackets, and tell the user where the cleanse diverged from the original so
  they can choose proven-original vs. AI-clean per email.
- Every `{Testimonial}`, `{Customer Name}`, `{X}` count, `{$X}` value, and
  `{Link}` is data-dependent. If it is not real and verified, HOLD it per the
  HOLD pattern — do not ship fabricated proof.
- Subject lines here are starting points. Refine each against the 5 subject-line
  rules in `references/fomo-campaign.md` Module #4.
